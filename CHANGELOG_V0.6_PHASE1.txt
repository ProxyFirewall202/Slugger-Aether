Slugger NEO v0.6 — Phase 1 changelog

ECONOMY
- Removed Cells and Purified Warp from the active save schema and rewards.
- Removed weapon levels and their damage multipliers.
- Removed item reinforcement ranks and rank-based stat multipliers.
- Existing obsolete save fields/ranks are discarded during normalization, with no refunds.
- Removed old resource and rank actions from Singleplayer and PvP upgrade UI.

CRATES & DECRYPTION
- Added Crates & Decryption, Key Crafting Terminal, and Quantum Forge sub-tabs.
- Owned crate collection displays only crate types with a positive count, plus the currently opening crate.
- Drop-rate popup also shows only owned crates.
- Added graphical previews to crate cards and a separate area to purchase Wood/Iron crates.
- Removed old Cells/Purified Warp crate drop entries.

LOADOUT
- Added Equip Slot Grid, Weapon Stats & DPS Rankings, and Sets & Progress subtabs.
- Equipment collection grouped into weapon, shield, armor, boost/charm, and spirit slug categories.
- Added SVG previews to equipped slots, equipment cards, weapon rankings, and set piece tracking.

PVP / HUD
- Repositioned the 1v1 upgrade status and timer into its own responsive strip, avoiding the top HUD resource display.
- PvP loadouts no longer transmit weapon reinforcement or weapon-level fields.

DEFERRED
- 30+ specialized crates, crate discovery and nested rarity tiers remain Phase 2.
- Unique per-item SVG behaviour, animated armour shell plates, wave background motion, and online friend toasts remain Phase 3.
- No Cloudflare Worker changes in this release.
